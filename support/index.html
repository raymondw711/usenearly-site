Support

Need help with Nearly? Email support@usenearly.app.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete account. That deletes your account and the purchase records linked to it on our server, and everything Nearly keeps on this iPhone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Plus subscription. Cancel that through Apple first, as described below.

To wipe what's on this iPhone without deleting your account, go to Settings › Your data and choose “Delete all data on this iPhone.” Signing out or deleting the app does not delete your account on our server.

You can also email support@usenearly.app to ask us to access, correct or delete your data. We respond within 45 days.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Plus is billed through Apple. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings, or tap Manage subscriptions in Nearly's Settings, which opens Apple's subscription page. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Why does Nearly need location permission?

Nearly uses your position on your iPhone to tell when a place you've chosen is nearby, and to alert you. If you allow “Always”, the app can alert you while it's closed. Matching runs on your iPhone. We don't keep a location history, and your exact location stays on your iPhone. You can turn off location at any time in the iPhone's Settings.
